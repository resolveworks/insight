# Chat

Ask questions in plain language, then use follow-ups to narrow a search or examine a result in more detail.

## Questions you can ask

- “What was the total budget for 2023?”
- “Find all mentions of Company X.”
- “Compare the 2020 and 2023 assessments.”
- “Summarize the main findings of these reports.”

## Check before publishing

AI-generated answers can be incomplete or wrong. Use the citations to find the underlying documents, then verify important facts and quotations against the originals.

Conversations are stored only on your computer. Sharing a collection shares its documents, not its chats. See [Getting Started](./getting-started.md#choose-an-ai-provider) for what your AI provider receives.
